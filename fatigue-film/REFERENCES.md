# Sources and photograph credits

Reviewed 30 October 2026. These sources support the teaching concepts; they are not the source of the synthetic S-N numbers.

1. **David Roylance, MIT (2001), Fatigue.** Repeated loading, initiation/growth, S-N diagrams, scatter and cyclic crack growth. PDF pp. 1-4 and 6-7.
   https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/f678e01017186c53d45845ad93c07938_MIT3_11F99_fatigue.pdf

2. **L. Anand and D. M. Parks, MIT 2.002 (2004), Defect-Free Fatigue.** Stress amplitude and stress-life curves, including material-dependent endurance limits. PDF pp. 14 and 17-19.
   https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-002-mechanics-and-materials-ii-spring-2004/db45866e2fc0cf164d96828936748e8f_lec21_notes.pdf

3. **David Roylance, MIT (2001), Introduction to Fracture Mechanics.** Stress intensity, geometry, toughness and linear-elastic applicability.
   https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/e5bca953e1dbd0cd0376f0f77033dc0d_MIT3_11F99_frac.pdf

4. **NASA-HDBK-5010, Volume 1, Revision A (2023), Fracture Control Implementation Handbook for Spaceflight Hardware.** Definition of K_Ic and validity requirements, printed/PDF p. 25.
   https://standards.nasa.gov/sites/default/files/standards/NASA/Revision/0/2023-12-14-NASA-HDBK-5010A-Vol-1-Rev-Approved.pdf

5. **NIST, SEM images of AM titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V) high-cycle fatigue fracture surfaces.** Uploaded 7 February 2017. The white arrow identifies crack initiation at a lack-of-fusion defect; black arrows identify pores. Used at 00:23-00:33. Resized, with original arrows and scale bars retained.
   https://www.nist.gov/image/semti-6al-4vpng-0
   Original image: https://www.nist.gov/sites/default/files/images/2017/02/07/semti-6al-4v_1.png
   NIST reuse policy: https://www.nist.gov/copyrights-disclaimers
   The image page shows no separate copyright marking. NIST permits copying unmarked site information with appropriate credit.

6. **Oxensepp (4 January 2014), 0x-kurbel-def.jpg.** Own-work photograph of a broken bicycle crank. The source description identifies fatigue growth and sudden fracture. Used at 02:31-02:40; resized and annotated with arrows and region labels.
   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:0x-kurbel-def.jpg
   Original image: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/03/0x-kurbel-def.jpg
   Chosen license: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported.
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

All numerical S-N points and scatter are created for teaching. None is a crank measurement. The photographed crank is not assigned an unverified alloy, stress, crack size, load history or life.

The bicycle illustration, specimen schematics, curves and animations are drawn with Python/Pillow. Formulas use Matplotlib mathtext. English narration uses Microsoft Edge's en-US-AriaNeural voice via edge-tts. The source package includes the rendered narration so offline video reconstruction does not need the speech service.
